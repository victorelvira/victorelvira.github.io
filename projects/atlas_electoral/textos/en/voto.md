## Every province {#todas | Every province}

[sub] The best option of each bloc in each province, and how often 1,000 more votes for that party give the bloc one more seat. Click a header to sort.

[[lista]]

::: metodología
For each province and each simulation of «Our forecast» (140,000), we count how many more votes each list needs to win one more seat under the D'Hondt rule and the 3% threshold, and whom it would take it from. A bundle of 1,000 new votes for a party gives the bloc one more seat when that seat comes from another bloc: if the PP takes it from Vox, the right gains nothing. Figures are out of 1,000 simulations. What helps is comparing the parties within your province; across provinces, small ones come out higher because 1,000 votes are a larger part of the total there. It assumes everyone else votes as forecast, and it is not voting advice: it describes the forecast, with its uncertainty. The blocs are the starting groups of «Build your majority»; in Navarra, Geroa Bai counts with the PNV.
:::
