Sports Atlas
Open data: all tables as CSV · datapackage.json · how they join. Matches, draws, squads and tables read from the English Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0); places from Wikidata (CC0); World Cup scorers checked against the Fjelstul World Cup Database (© 2023 Joshua C. Fjelstul, Ph.D., CC BY-SA 4.0); league dates also from James Curley's engsoccerdata (GPL); tennis draws checked against, and ATP and WTA ranks from, Jeff Sackmann / Tennis Abstract's tennis_atp and tennis_wta (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Flags from Wikimedia Commons (almost all public domain; each file's licence is recorded). Club crests belong to their clubs and are shown, as Wikipedia shows them, to identify the club the atlas informs about; any will be removed on request. Where a source does not add up, the atlas says so instead of guessing.
